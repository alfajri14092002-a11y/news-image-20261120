Lead story • Europe

EU–UK summit opens in Brussels

Prime Minister Andy Burnham meets Ursula von der Leyen and António Costa at the second post-Brexit summit. Deals to cut checks on food and farm exports, link emissions trading, and a youth mobility scheme for young Britons and Europeans. London values the package at £5.1 billion a year.

Wall Street slides 2% as AI rally fades

The Dow falls 1,101 points to 49,097 and the S&P 500 logs a sixth straight loss, its worst week since April 2025. Nvidia drops 3.3% as traders price in a likely Fed rate rise in December.

Anthropic’s record $81bn IPO debuts

The Claude-maker lists on Nasdaq in the largest IPO in history, valuing the firm at about $1.5 trillion. Shares open at $111.50, touch $115, and close at $97.17 — still 17.1% above the offer price.

Students walk out across Europe

Walkouts in the UK and more than 25 countries on the International Youth Day of Action, against rearmament and for education, welfare and climate.

Cyclone Akvan bears down on Odisha

The severe storm is due to make landfall on 20 November; 80,000 people have been moved to safety and schools are closed along the Odisha and Andhra coasts.

Phillips ton puts New Zealand on top

Glenn Phillips hits 118 — after a ball struck his helmet — and Henry Nicholls is 146 not out as New Zealand reach 317–6 at lunch on day two in Wellington.
